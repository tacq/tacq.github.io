Privacy Policy — Astra Wing / 星翼战姬

Overview: Astra Wing / 星翼战姬 (com.tacq.cyberwing) is a vertical sci-fi shoot-'em-up game for Android and iOS that can be played fully offline. We (the developer) do not run any servers and do not collect your personal data ourselves. The game has no user accounts of its own and no analytics of its own. Optional platform services are built in: rewarded video ads (Google AdMob), which play only when you choose to watch one, leaderboards (Google Play Games Services on Android, Apple Game Center on iOS), and optional tips / Supporter Pack purchases through Google Play or the App Store. These services collect data as described below.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your campaign progress, star ratings, hangar upgrades, unlocked airframes, codex records, audio preferences, language selection, and a randomly generated anonymous Player ID are stored only on your device.

  • This data is never uploaded to us. Only if you sign in to Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS) are your leaderboard scores sent to Google or Apple (section 4).
  • Erasing Local Data: Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Astra Wing permanently deletes all local save data, including the Player ID.

2. Optional Tips & Supporter Pack (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store)

Astra Wing includes an optional "Support the Dev" sheet: consumable tips and a one-time Supporter Pack. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing (Android) or the Apple App Store / StoreKit (iOS). We never receive, view, or store your name, billing address, card number, or other payment details. The store only tells the game which product was purchased (and, for the Supporter Pack, whether you still own it, so it can be restored on a new install and removed after a refund); this check happens on your device and is never sent to us. Payment information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

3. Optional Rewarded Ads (Google AdMob)

Astra Wing never shows banner or interstitial ads. A short video ad plays only when you choose one (revive once near a boss kill, double a sortie's star cores, or +2 lives when you run out). To serve, measure and protect these ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / app set ID, or the iOS IDFA if you allow tracking) and vendor ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Consent: where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states) the game asks for your consent with Google's consent message, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Tracking (iOS): the iPhone asks with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the IDFA can be used; you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID (Android): you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for teens (max rating T).

4. Optional Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services / Apple Game Center)

If you sign in to Google Play Games from the RANKING screen, your scores (daily / weekly / boss-gauntlet results, heat chain, total stars and total takedowns) are uploaded to Google's leaderboards together with your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), which other players can see on the leaderboards. Play Games is operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; you can manage your profile visibility and delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app. If you never sign in, scores stay on your device only.

On iOS the same scores go to Apple Game Center when you are signed in to Game Center on your device, together with your Game Center player ID and nickname, which other players can see on the leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; you can sign out or change your profile in Settings → Game Center. Signed out, scores stay on your device only.

5. Share & Report a Bug

If you tap Share or Report a Bug, the game only opens your own browser, email app, or system share sheet with pre-filled text. Bug reports include your anonymous Player ID and basic device information (such as Android / iOS version, screen size, and game version) to help troubleshoot. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it yourself, and you can review or edit the text first.

6. Children's Privacy

Astra Wing is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If a future update adds online features or changes how data is handled, we will update this page, the Google Play Data Safety declaration and the App Store privacy details before that update is published.

8. Contact & Support

For privacy questions, support requests, or bug reports, email zrl0420@gmail.com.
