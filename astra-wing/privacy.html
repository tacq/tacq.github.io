Privacy Policy — Astra Wing / 星翼战姬

Overview: Astra Wing / 星翼战姬 (com.tacq.cyberwing) is an offline vertical sci-fi shoot-'em-up game for Android. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. The game has no user accounts, no advertisements, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking, and it does not send any data off your device.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your campaign progress, star ratings, hangar upgrades, unlocked airframes, codex records, audio preferences, language selection, and a randomly generated anonymous Player ID are stored only on your device.

  • This data is never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Erasing Local Data: Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Astra Wing permanently deletes all local save data, including the Player ID.

2. Optional Tips (Google Play Billing)

Astra Wing includes an optional "Support the Dev" tip jar. Tips are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive, view, or store your name, billing address, card number, or other payment details. Google Play only tells the game which tip was purchased, so it can show a thank-you message. Google's handling of payment information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Share & Report a Bug

If you tap Share or Report a Bug, the game only opens your own browser, email app, or system share sheet with pre-filled text. Bug reports include your anonymous Player ID and basic device information (such as Android version, screen size, and game version) to help troubleshoot. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it yourself, and you can review or edit the text first.

4. Children's Privacy

Astra Wing is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes to This Policy

If a future update adds online features or changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the Google Play Data Safety declaration before that update is published.

6. Contact & Support

For privacy questions, support requests, or bug reports, email zrl0420@gmail.com.
