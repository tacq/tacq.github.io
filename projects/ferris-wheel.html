Ferris Wheel Photo Frame

A custom rotating Ferris wheel photo frame designed for my daughter, Cora, capable of displaying up to 12 family photos across six double-sided swinging bunny-eared gondolas. The wheel frame, picket fence courtyard, and miniature characters (rabbit, balloon bear, and unicorns) are 3D printed — with the characters hand-painted and the frame detailed one by one with tiny nail-art flowers and butterflies.

Overview

Between robotics and software projects, I wanted to build something warm and personal for my daughter, Cora — a tabletop Ferris wheel that turns everyday family snapshots into a miniature amusement park scene.

As the wheel spins, six gravity-leveled gondolas swing gently so that the photos always stay upright. Each gondola holds two back-to-back prints, allowing the wheel to showcase up to 12 photos at once.

3D printing, hand painting & nail-art detailing

🎡

12-photo rotating wheel

Twin star-spoked wheel rims, A-frame support legs, and six bunny-eared double-sided photo gondolas printed in crisp white PLA.

🎨

Hand-painted 3D miniatures

The decorative rabbit on the hub, the balloon bear on the lower arch, and the two unicorns in the courtyard are 3D printed and hand-painted.

🌸

Nail-art flower & butterfly trim

Miniature 3D pastel roses, leaves, and butterflies — originally made for women's nail art — were purchased and hand-glued one by one across the wheel.

🏡

Fenced courtyard & nameplate

Surrounded by a 3D-printed white picket fence base and crowned with a custom cora center medallion framed by a floral wreath.
