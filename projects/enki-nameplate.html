ENKI Motion-Activated Illuminated Nameplate

A custom wall-mounted nameplate built for my son, Enki, in 2014 — blending hands-on woodworking, 3ds Max 3D design, two-part 3D-printed diffuser letters, and motion-triggered LED electronics. Inspired by a decorative slatted plaque I saw at Target, the sign automatically lights up for 30 seconds whenever someone walks past.

Overview & inspiration

While browsing Target, I spotted a slatted wooden wall plaque and had the idea to build a personalized, illuminated version for my son, Enki. Instead of a static sign, I wanted it to double as a gentle hallway/doorway nightlight that comes alive automatically when someone walks by.

The finished sign combines a custom-cut plywood backboard and warm natural wood slats with bold geometric E N K I lettering modeled in 3ds Max. Inside each hollow 3D-printed character sits a cluster of LEDs wired together and controlled by a concealed motion detection sensor that lights the nameplate for 30 seconds on every pass.

Design & build process

📐

1. 3ds Max typography & 2-part letters

Designed custom stencil-style E N K I letterforms in 3ds Max. Each letter is engineered as two 3D-printed parts: a matte-black rear housing channel and a snap-fit translucent white front diffuser cap.

🪚

2. Plywood cutting & slatted woodwork

Cut the main backing plate from plywood using a power saw (电锯), finished it in matte black for high contrast, and mounted six evenly spaced natural wood horizontal slats across the face.

💡

3. Internal per-letter LED wiring

Embedded bright warm-white LEDs inside the cavity of each 3D-printed letter before snapping on the white diffuser caps, then routed and connected all four letters together behind the slats.

📡

4. 30-second PIR motion trigger

Integrated a passive infrared (PIR) motion sensor into the circuit so walking past the bedroom door automatically illuminates the entire sign for 30 seconds before powering back down.
