Privacy Policy

GreenGrid: Living City

Bundle / Package ID: com.tacq.ecocity · Publisher: Enco Universe · Effective date: November 13, 2026

GreenGrid: Living City is an idle resource-network and sustainable city-balancing puzzle game for iOS and Android. We respect your privacy and collect the minimum data needed to run the game, deliver optional level packs, process optional in-app purchases, and serve ads where enabled.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Game Progress & Settings: Level completion, unlocks, language preference, audio/accessibility settings, and local event logs are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our own servers.
  • Over-the-Air Level Packs: The app periodically checks static HTTPS files hosted on GitHub Pages (tacq.github.io) to download signed, data-only level updates. No user account or personal identifier is sent in these requests.

2. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob)

Free versions of the game may display infrequent interstitial ads between completed levels and opt-in rewarded ads when you request a hint or bonus. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA when permitted), coarse IP-based location, ad interaction events, and diagnostic information to serve ads and prevent fraud.

  • EEA / UK / Switzerland (GDPR): We present Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before initializing ads, and you can review or withdraw consent anytime via Settings → Privacy Options inside the game.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, cross-app tracking identifiers (IDFA) are accessed only if you grant permission in Apple's system prompt.
  • See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases (such as Remove Ads, product ID remove_ads) are processed directly by Apple App Store (StoreKit 2) or Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or payment details, and you can restore owned non-consumable purchases anytime via Settings → Restore Purchases.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ad requests are configured with family-safe content ratings (PG / general audiences).

5. Your Rights & Contact

You can delete all local game data at any time via Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app. For privacy questions or support requests, please visit our Support & Contact page.
