How it plays

Smart balance beats the biggest upgrade

Every building has a trade-off you can see before you buy. Read the city, fix the bottleneck, and watch the numbers turn green.

⚡

Five systems, one living city

Power, materials, waste, recycling and roads all feed each other. Solar dips at night, batteries carry you through, and a jammed road slows everything. Tap any building to see why it runs at its current speed.

🎯

24 goals in 2 acts

Clear goals one at a time to unlock new buildings and islands. Act 2 takes your city to the coast with new tidal power, harbour logistics and a fresh set of challenges.

🧪

Research & upgrades

Spend research points on improvements such as cleaner recycling, faster cranes or stronger solar panels, and plan upgrades with the now / next-level stats side by side.

🌙

Relaxing, at your pace

Your city keeps working while you're away, and offline earnings wait for you when you return. Claim daily rewards, follow the friendly guided tutorial and play in short, calm sessions.

Availability, privacy & support

Coming soon

GreenGrid: Living City is coming soon to the App Store (iPhone) and Google Play (Android). Store links will appear here at launch.

Free, with fair ads

The game is free. Rewarded ads, such as doubling offline earnings or a short boost, only play when you choose them, and interstitial ads are infrequent. An optional one-time Remove Ads purchase removes interstitial ads for good.

Your privacy

No account and no sign-up. Your city and settings are saved on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
