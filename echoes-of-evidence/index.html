How it plays

Think like a detective, not a guesser

Every case is checked by a logic engine, so there is exactly one answer and you can always reason your way to it.

🗂️

Read the evidence

Door logs, camera stills, messages, floor plans, walking-time maps and witness statements. Records are always true; people can be wrong.

🕰️

Rebuild the timeline

Drag events into order. Check board is free and unlimited: it lists every conflict with the cards you've read, in plain language.

⚖️

Compare & conclude

Put two cards side by side to see if they contradict each other and why. Then answer the case questions: who did it, what happened just before, and which statement can't be true.

🌧️

Fair hints, cozy noir

Hints only unlock once you're stuck, and they point at cards, never at the answer. Three chapters of small, non-violent mysteries, from riverside mornings to a night train.

Availability, privacy & support

Coming soon

Echoes of Evidence is coming soon to the App Store (iPhone) and Google Play (Android). Store links will appear here at launch.

Free, with fair ads

The game is free. Ads never appear while you work a case: an occasional ad may play between cases, and rewarded ads only play when you choose one. An optional one-time Remove Ads purchase removes interstitial ads for good.

Your privacy

No account and no sign-up. Progress and settings are saved on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
