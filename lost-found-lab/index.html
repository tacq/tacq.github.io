How it plays

Look closer. Think it through. Send it home.

Each case is a small logic puzzle built from real clues: initials, locker numbers, colours, tags and notes.

🔍

Hands-on inspecting

Tap glowing spots to read clues, flip objects to see the back, scrub away grime, fit a missing piece into its socket and slot in a battery to bring a gadget back to life.

🧩

Fair, clue-based logic

Owner cards tick what matches and cross what doesn't, always with a shape and a word, never colour alone. Route an object wrong and the game shows exactly which clue contradicts it.

🏛️

18 cases, 3 places

Start at the Community Pool, move on to the Train Station, then open the Science Museum wing with fossils, microscopes, magnets and more. Every case is handmade.

💡

Your lab, your pace

Earn credits to upgrade the lab, fill your collection shelf with every object you return and claim daily rewards. Hints wake up only after you've been stuck a while, and they point at evidence, never at the answer.

Availability, privacy & support

Coming soon

The Lost & Found Lab is coming soon to the App Store (iPhone) and Google Play (Android). Store links will appear here at launch.

Free, with fair ads

The game is free. Ads never appear during a puzzle: an occasional ad may play between cases, and rewarded ads only play when you choose one. An optional one-time Remove Ads purchase removes interstitial ads for good.

Your privacy

No account and no sign-up. Progress and settings are saved on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
