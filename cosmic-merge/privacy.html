Privacy Policy — Cosmic Merge: Planet Drop

Overview: Cosmic Merge: Planet Drop (com.tacq.cosmicmerge, built with Godot 4.7 for Android and iOS, published by Enco Universe) is a casual puzzle game: drop planets into a jar and merge matching planets into bigger ones. We do not require you to create an account with us and we do not operate our own backend servers to store your personal data.

1. Local Device Data & Player ID

Your best score, audio and vibration settings, language preference, "Remove Ads" / Ad-Free reward status, the daily free booster date, and a randomly generated local Player ID (formatted as CM-XXXXXX) are stored strictly locally on your device.

  • Local Player ID (CM-XXXXXX): Generated locally on your device and used solely to identify your bug reports and to verify Ad-Free reward codes we may send you. It is never transmitted unless you voluntarily include it when you send us a bug report or feedback email.
  • Erasing Local Data: All local save data and your local Player ID can be erased at any time by clearing the app's storage data or uninstalling Cosmic Merge from your device.

2. Third-Party Services We Use

Cosmic Merge integrates official platform and monetization SDKs provided by Google and Apple to support advertising, an optional leaderboard, and an optional in-app purchase:

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Cosmic Merge displays interstitial ads (only after a game over, with frequency limits) and opt-in rewarded video ads via Google AdMob, unless you have an active Ad-Free reward or have purchased the "Remove Ads" upgrade. There are no banner ads and no ads when the app starts.
  • AdMob may automatically collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA), coarse location data (derived from IP address), app interaction data (such as app launch and ad views/clicks), and diagnostic/crash data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and measure ad performance.
  • We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage GDPR, UK, Swiss, and US state privacy consent choices. On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown before any tracking; if you decline, the IDFA is not used.
  • Learn more about how Google handles data in its partner services:
    • Google Privacy Policy
    • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Leaderboard (Google Play Games Services & Apple Game Center)

When you use the optional Ranking / Leaderboard feature, your best score and public gamer profile/ID are handled directly by Google Play Games Services (on Android) or Apple Game Center (on iOS) under their respective privacy policies (Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy).

In-App Purchase (Google Play Billing & Apple StoreKit)

The optional "Remove Ads" non-consumable purchase and purchase restorations are processed securely and directly by Google Play Billing (on Android) or Apple App Store / StoreKit (on iOS). We never receive, process, or store your payment card or banking details.

3. Your Privacy Choices & Consent (GDPR / EEA / UK & US State Privacy)

Players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), Switzerland, and applicable US states are presented with a consent dialog (via Google's User Messaging Platform) on first launch.

  • In-Game Privacy Settings: Where required, you can review, change, or withdraw your consent at any time inside the game via Home → Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Device-Level Controls: You can also reset or delete your device's Advertising ID, limit ad tracking, or change the tracking permission (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) in your device's system settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Cosmic Merge is intended for a general audience (ages 13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not run our own backend servers or user accounts, we do not store personal records on our servers. Local save data and your local Player ID can be deleted at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. If you send us a voluntary bug report or feedback email, the information you provide is used solely to answer you and troubleshoot the issue, and you can ask us to delete that email at any time.
