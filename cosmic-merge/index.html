Cosmic Merge: Planet Drop

A relaxing space puzzle — drop cute planets into the jar and merge matching pairs into bigger planets. Plays offline, no account needed.

🪐 Merge

Two equal planets that touch merge into the next of 11 sizes.

⚡ Chain combos

Quick merges in a row multiply your points.

🕳️ Gravity Wells

Black holes pull planets together and swallow the tiniest ones.

🌟 Supernova

Merge two of the biggest planets for a huge bonus.

🎮 How to Play

  1. Tap Play on the home screen.
  2. Drag to aim and release to drop a planet. The next planet is shown in advance.
  3. Two equal planets that touch merge into a bigger one. Keep the jar from overflowing — if planets stay above the line, the run ends.
  4. Sweep clears the smallest planets (up to 3 per run, the first one each day is free). After a game over you can Continue once per run.

❓ FAQ

Remove Ads & Restore Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase on the home screen. It removes the interstitial ads and gives you Sweep and Continue without watching videos. Reinstalled the game or got a new phone? Tap Restore Purchases on the home screen, signed in with the same Apple ID / Google account.

When do ads appear?

Never at launch and never during a run. An interstitial may appear only after a game over (at most once every few games). Rewarded videos are always optional.

Leaderboard

Tap Ranking on the home screen. Scores are submitted to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) when you are signed in; otherwise your local best is shown.

Privacy choices & your data

Change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy Options (shown where required by law). Your progress is stored only on your device; uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

✉️ Contact Support & Report a Bug

The quickest way: open Cosmic Merge → Settings (⚙) → Report a Bug and tap Send Email — your device details and Player ID (CM-XXXXXX) are attached automatically. You can also email us directly; please describe what happened and, if possible, include your Player ID from Settings → Report a Bug.
