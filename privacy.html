Privacy Policy — Pocket Arcade

Overview: Pocket Arcade (com.tacq.pocketarcade, built with Godot 4.7 for Android and iOS) is a collection of four casual arcade mini-games: Cosmic Merge, Gem Blocks, Sky Stack, and Pin Spin. We do not require you to create an account with us and we do not operate our own backend servers to store your personal data.

1. Local Device Data & Player ID

High scores, game progress, audio and vibration settings, language preferences, "Remove Ads" / Ad-Free Reward Pass status, and a randomly generated local Player ID (formatted as PA-XXXXXX) are stored strictly locally on your device.

  • Local Player ID (PA-XXXXXX): Generated locally on your device and used solely to issue and verify 3-Day Ad-Free Bug Bounty reward codes (e.g., PA3-XXXX-YYYYYY). It is never transmitted unless you voluntarily include it when submitting a bug report.
  • Erasing Local Data: All local save data and your local Player ID can be erased at any time by clearing the app's storage data or uninstalling Pocket Arcade from your device.

2. Third-Party Services We Use

Pocket Arcade integrates official platform and monetization SDKs provided by Google and Apple to support advertising, optional leaderboards, and optional in-app purchases:

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Pocket Arcade displays banner, interstitial, and opt-in rewarded ads via Google AdMob, unless you have an active Ad-Free Reward Pass or have purchased the "Remove Ads" upgrade.
  • AdMob may automatically collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA), coarse location data (derived from IP address), app interaction data (such as app launch and ad views/clicks), and diagnostic/crash data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and measure ad performance.
  • We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage GDPR, UK, Swiss, and US state privacy consent choices.
  • Learn more about how Google handles data in its partner services:
    • Google Privacy Policy
    • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services & Apple Game Center)

When signed into the optional Ranking / Leaderboard feature, your game scores and public gamer profile/ID are handled directly by Google Play Games Services (on Android) or Apple Game Center (on iOS) under their respective privacy policies (Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy).

In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing & Apple StoreKit)

Optional "Remove Ads" non-consumable purchases and purchase restorations are processed securely and directly by Google Play Billing (on Android) or Apple App Store / StoreKit (on iOS). We never receive, process, or store your payment card or banking details.

3. Your Privacy Choices & Consent (GDPR / EEA / UK & US State Privacy)

Players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), Switzerland, and applicable US states are presented with a consent dialog (via Google's User Messaging Platform) on first launch.

  • In-Game Privacy Settings: You can review, change, or withdraw your consent at any time inside the game via Home → Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Device-Level Controls: You can also reset or delete your device's Advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your device's system settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Pocket Arcade is intended for a general audience (ages 13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not run our own backend servers or user accounts, we do not store personal records on our servers. Local save data and your local Player ID can be deleted at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. If you submit a voluntary bug report via GitHub Issues or email, the information you provide is used solely to troubleshoot the issue and issue your Bug Bounty reward code.
