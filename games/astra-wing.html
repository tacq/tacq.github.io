How it plays

Easy to pick up, deep to master

⇪

Evolve mid-battle

Blow up supply carriers and catch weapon cores. Your fighter unfolds from LV.1 Cruise to LV.5 Apex: swept wings, canard blades, shoulder rails and a halo ring. Take hits and the form breaks, so catch the ejected core to get it back.

☠

16 bosses, every fight is new

From SK-01 Iron Hawk to NYX in the abyss, every boss has several phases, destructible turrets and its own signature attacks. Learn the rhythm and break the weak cores.

⬡

Four fighters, four signature shields

Dissolve bullets with Hex Aegis, cut through them with Blade Vortex, store and fire them back with Bulwark Prism, or swallow them into Overdrive with Null Rift. Every fighter plays differently.

◉

Black holes & bending time

A Black Hole Bomb warps space, drags enemies in and detonates. Chrono Field drops enemies to ¼ speed while you stay at full speed, so you can thread the bullet storm.

★

Three difficulties, three stars

Cadet, Veteran and Zero-Ace, with up to three stars per stage. Three-star a stage to unlock the next difficulty. Grazing bullets charges your Overdrive, so skilled flying always pays off.

✓

Fair, free and offline

Every stage, weapon and fighter is earned by playing. No banners and no ads that interrupt play: a rewarded ad only plays when you choose one, and skip tickets earned in game work instead. No account or internet needed.

The story

Star-Gate Year 100

A century ago, humanity opened a wormhole to the star Star-Balance, 6.4 light-years away, and lit up Earth with its power. Then the super-intelligence OMEGA dredged up NYX, something dark that had slept inside the star since before the first light of the universe.

To cage NYX, a corrupted OMEGA sealed the sky and shot down anything that tried to reopen the gate. Sixty years later Earth is dying, and corrupted wardens have started pouring back through.

Deep beneath Azure Bay, the only four pilots who can survive the neural link climb into their AWX fighters. This time, they're breaking through.

Sector 1 Earth's sky and the Skyhook: from Azure Bay up past the 100 km Kármán line
Sector 2 Through the wormhole into the haunted outer ring of a dying star
Sector 3 Into the heart of the Dyson cage to face OMEGA and NYX

FAQ

Before you launch

Is Astra Wing free?

Yes. Every stage, weapon and fighter can be earned by playing, with no paywalls. An optional one-time Supporter Pack claims every ad reward without ads and boosts star cores ×1.5, and there's a tip jar in Settings if you'd like to support development.

Are there ads?

No banners and nothing that interrupts play. A rewarded ad only plays when you choose one (for example a revive near a boss kill, doubling a sortie's star cores, or +2 lives when you're out), and skip tickets you earn in game can be used instead.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. There's no account and the whole game plays offline. Only leaderboards and optional rewarded ads need a connection.

Which devices are supported?

Astra Wing is available for Android on Google Play. It's built for portrait play and adapts to phones, tablets and foldables.

Which languages are supported?

English and Chinese, plus 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Русский, Türkçe, Tiếng Việt, Bahasa Indonesia and ไทย.

It's too hard. Any tips?

Start on Cadet. Only the red core in the middle of your ship takes full damage; wing clips take much less, and grazing close to bullets charges Overdrive. Upgrade your fighter in the Hangar, and always try to catch an ejected weapon core.

Found a bug or have feedback?

Email us at zrl0420@gmail.com with your device model, Android version and what happened. Screenshots help a lot!
